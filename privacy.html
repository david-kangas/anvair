Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

ANVAIR respects your privacy. The ANVAIR application does not create user accounts, collect personal information, track users for advertising purposes, or sell user data.

HealthKit

ANVAIR uses Apple HealthKit to create and save workout sessions to the Apple Health app. Health and workout information remains under the control of the user and is managed by Apple Health.

ANVAIR does not transmit HealthKit data to external servers or third parties.

ANVAIR Application Data

The ANVAIR application does not collect personal information, advertising identifiers, location information, or app usage analytics.

Website Analytics

The ANVAIR website uses optional Google Analytics to help understand how visitors use the site. Analytics may include information such as pages viewed, interactions with the site, approximate geographic region, device or browser information, and how a visitor arrived at the website.

Google Analytics is not loaded unless the visitor chooses to accept analytics. Visitors may decline analytics and continue using the website normally. The visitor's analytics preference is stored in the browser so that the choice can be remembered on future visits.

ANVAIR does not use Google Analytics for personalized advertising or advertising profiling. Advertising-related consent is denied by default.

Visitors may review or change their analytics choice at any time by selecting Analytics Preferences.

Third-Party Services

The ANVAIR website uses Google Analytics only when a visitor has chosen to permit analytics. Google may process analytics information in accordance with its own privacy practices.

ANVAIR does not use advertising networks or sell website visitor information.
